About this map

This is the NextXus Federation God's Eye world map. It shows live events from three free public sources: earthquakes from the last 24 hours from the US Geological Survey, open storms and wildfires from NASA EONET, and current disaster alerts from GDACS, the Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System.

Press the TEXT LIST button to hear every event as a list with links. Press ASK ROGER SIM to ask the Federation's Roger Sim about what is happening. No account and no key are needed. Nothing is tracked.

Sources: USGS earthquakes, NASA EONET, GDACS. Back to God's Eye.
